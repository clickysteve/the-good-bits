✦ automatic sample chopper

The Good Bits

Finds candidate musical phrases and loop-ready drum breaks in long source recordings — a fast first-pass editor, not a final chopper. All processing happens locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Mode

🎷

Sax / Trumpet

Pulls natural phrases from pauses in solo horn performances.

🎹

Rhodes

Conservative — keeps whole chord progressions and passages together.

🥁

Drums

Break-sized loops snapped to the detected tempo, plus optional one-shot hits.

Uses the detected tempo to compute bar length in seconds; falls back to a fixed length if tempo isn't detected.
